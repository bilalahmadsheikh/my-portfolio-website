The Story

"Civilizations rise when knowledge becomes creation."

Knowledge was once invention, discovery, and architecture — people built to move civilization forward. Today, our best thinking disappears inside platforms: AI conversations vanish, browser contexts evaporate, ideas die in closed tabs. Project Golden Age exists to reverse that — tools for research, writing, and memory, each one owned by you, not rented from a platform.

The Creed

Knowledge should empower people,
not disappear inside platforms.

First Product

gulaq

A Chrome extension + desktop vault. It saves your browser workspaces and your ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity & Copilot conversations to your own computer — searchable, exportable, and restorable in one click. The core is free forever.
